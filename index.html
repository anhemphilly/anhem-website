Our Core Philosophy

“‘Anh Em’ translates to ‘Brothers and Family.’ We were built on the conviction that the finest meals are born from heritage, crafted with precision, and shared in good company.”

At Anh Em Bar & Kitchen, our mission is to elevate traditional Vietnamese street food and classic home-style dishes into a refined dining experience. By uniting time-honored recipes with atmospheric mixology and warm hospitality, we bring the true spirit of Vietnamese family dining to Philadelphia.

Signature Creations

Starter

Bò Tái Chanh

Lime-cured, thinly sliced raw sirloin topped with garlic, Thai chili, fried shallots, cilantro, coriander, crushed peanuts, and house fish sauce.

Bánh

Bánh Xèo

Savory crispy crepe stuffed with cabbage, bean sprouts, onion, lettuce, and Asian herbs. Served with a tangy fish sauce vinaigrette and your choice of shrimp, pork & shrimp, or tofu.

Rice Plate

Thịt Kho

Comforting braised pork belly and hard-boiled eggs served with fragrant jasmine rice and house pickled cucumbers (Gluten-Free).

Starter

Cánh Gà Chiên

Crispy fried wingettes tossed in your choice of signature fish sauce glaze, Thai chili, or aromatic salt & pepper.

Salad

Watercress Beef Salad

Charbroiled beef set over fresh watercress, pickled onions, carrot, daikon, tomato, and hard-boiled egg with fish sauce vinaigrette.

Bánh

Bánh Mì (Viet Hoagie)

Mushroom pate, garlic butter, pickled carrot & daikon, cucumber, cilantro, and jalapeno on a crisp baguette with choice of char siu, beef, or tofu & bi.
